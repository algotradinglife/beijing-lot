北单 Combo Size Sweep — N ∈ {2..8}

Auto-generated · 8-year 3-SP dataset · niche-filtered + train/test split · fractional-Kelly with tax-cap split

1. Methodology

From the 94,395 qualifying single-leg picks generated by 15 OOS-validated niches (train: 期号<median; test: 期号≥median; survival ≡ test mean q > 1.064 with k≥30), we group leg picks by ISO week. For each N ∈ {2..8} and each week with K≥N qualifying picks (one option per match), we enumerate combos when C(K,N)≤5000 else sample 300. Each combo is sized via fractional Kelly (f=0.25·f*) with per-combo cap 0.5% bankroll, weekly stake cap 25% bankroll, then tax-cap split (per-ticket prize<9 999 yuan). PnL recompounds weekly only over the test half. Forward Monte Carlo (1000 paths × 52 weeks) bootstraps weekly PnL.

Caveats baked in: niche q estimates from the train half drive sizing; legs within a week are weakly correlated (weather, big-team coverage); the 7-leg case is data-limited (E[hits in 8 yrs] ≈ 0–1 even under positive edge), so its empirical numbers carry wide uncertainty.

8. Honest caveats

  • Small-n niches: some surviving niches had n_train just above 200; their q estimates are noisy.
  • Within-week correlation: combos in the same week share weather, refereeing, and TV-coverage shocks. Independence is approximate.
  • Selection bias residue: even with proper train→test split, the entire 15-niche grid was searched on the train half; the OOS survival filter limits but does not eliminate this.
  • Non-stationarity: payout structure and pool composition drift over 2018–2026 (Covid, new leagues). The forward MC assumes future weeks resemble past test-half weeks.
  • 7-leg data limit: under the modeled q, E[hits] in 8 years ≈ 0.03 — observed counts of 0 are consistent with both no-edge and edge+variance. Don't over-interpret.
  • Hit-rate vs predicted: deviation between observed hit_rate and ∏(q_train/SP) signals q inflation. Compare in master table column 4 vs 5.
