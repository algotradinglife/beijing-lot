# N=7 2023-2025 LL investigation

Date: 2026-06-27

## Question

The recent LL behavior deserves a separate check because `drop_LL` and LL haircuts lose badly in 2023-2025, while keyed base is positive.

This note investigates whether recent LL should be:

- kept;
- dropped;
- haircutted;
- treated differently by drift/pocket/topology.

3. The ticket constructor selects better LL legs than the full LL pool.
   Recent LL all-leg hit rate is `33.47%`; LL legs that are actually used by base tickets hit `38.47%`.

This is important:

- medium/low drift LL had much better hit rate and better EV proxy in 2023-2025.

So the earlier idea "keep LL when drift is high" is too simple. For LL specifically, high drift can still be a dead-ticket trap.

## Conclusion

LL deserves to stay in the search space, but not as a uniformly funded class.

What changed in 2023-2025 is not that LL became a high-hit class. Rather:

- LL hit rate stayed flat while HH/HL weakened;
- LL payout quality remained best;
- the base ticket constructor selected a better LL subset than the raw LL pool;
- hard LL removal broke a few jackpot topologies.

Recommended next step:

```text
Do not hard-drop LL.
Do not blindly keep all LL.
Split LL by pocket and drift:
  - keep/lightly haircut LL 0@-1;
  - keep cautiously LL 3@1, especially when needed for topology;
  - heavily haircut LL draw-tail 1@<=-2 unless independent confidence is strong;
  - do not treat high drift alone as a keep signal inside LL.
Add a max-LL-count / topology-aware depth gate:
  - prefer 0-2 LL legs per ticket;
  - allow >2 LL only when recent selector health is positive and pockets are mainly 0@-1.
```
