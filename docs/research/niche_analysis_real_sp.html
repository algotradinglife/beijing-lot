9. Caveats

  1. Selection bias on q: the "+78%" implied combo EV uses mean(did_win × SP) across the full niche. The picks you'd actually make use the same niche conditioned on a leg-EV filter, which shifts q downward (regression to the mean). The OOS test in §5 isolates this.
  2. Cross-correlation: 7 legs from different matches are independent in outcome, but SP movements within a week may share a public-favorite driver. Not modelled here.
  3. Bucket arbitrariness: alt-scheme parallel run shows whether niches survive a different cut (§5 footer).
  4. Survivor bias: previous −100% combo ROI was the "all combos lost" artifact. With real SP the sign and magnitude of ROI now reflects honest market exposure.
  5. SP semantics: Σ(1/SP) ≈ 1 here. If the goal is to study *retail* mispricing distinct from international fair-line, an additional comparison with avg_eu (Pinnacle) is needed; this analysis treats sp_3/sp_1/sp_0 as the bettable price.
