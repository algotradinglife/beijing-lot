1. Methodology

Each historical match contributes 3 binary observations (one per option ∈ {3,1,0}). For each option we compute an implied probability by de-vigging the avg-EU 1X2 odds (proportional method as primary; power method for sensitivity). We then enumerate ~hundreds of niches — strata defined by 1-, 2-, and 3-way combinations of option_type, handicap_class, option_strength, league_tier, prob_bucket.

For each niche we fit a Beta-Binomial posterior with prior mean = mean implied prob in the niche and concentration κ=100. The primary inclusion filter is P(true_p × SP_proxy × 0.65 > 1.05) ≥ 0.7: a distribution-aware threshold that incorporates niche-level uncertainty. SP proxy is the empirical median winning_sp in the niche when ≥10 wins exist, else mean(eu_odds) × 0.65 / 0.95.

9. Honest verdict

No actionable niche survives OOS

Found 4 niches passing the 70% credible threshold on full data; 6 on train, of which 0 survive OOS. Survival rate = 0.0%.

  • Caveat 1 (de-vig). Implied probs are approximate; sensitivity shows 4/20 agreement between methods.
  • Caveat 2 (within-niche heterogeneity). Implied prob varies within a niche; we use the in-niche mean as the comparator.
  • Caveat 3 (winner's curse). Filtering by P(EV>1.05) selects upward-biased niches by construction. The OOS test is the only honest corrective.
  • Caveat 4 (avg_eu vs 北单 SP). Even if avg_eu is mispriced, 北单 retail typically tracks avg_eu, so a "niche where avg_eu is wrong" is not necessarily "a niche where 北单 SP is wrong". The 35% rake on 北单 SP is the dominant economic constraint; only niches with very large posterior P(EV>1.05) and OOS confirmation are candidates for live testing.
  • Caveat 5 (small n). At n=50 the Beta posterior is ±10pp wide. P(EV>1.05)≥0.7 is meaningful only when SP_proxy is large enough that breakeven p is well inside the posterior support.
  • Caveat 6 (prior pull). With κ=100 and n≈60, the prior (centered at the in-niche mean implied prob) carries ~60% weight in the posterior. Inspecting the top niches: many have point hit rate well below implied (e.g. 0.57 vs 0.88), yet posterior mean stays high because the prior dominates. Their point EV is <1.05 even when P(EV>1.05) is >0.7. This is why we always cross-check OOS by point EV, not just posterior P(EV>threshold) — and why none of the train-passing niches survive the OOS point-EV test.
