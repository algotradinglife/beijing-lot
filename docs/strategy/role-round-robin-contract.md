Return groups, role_legs, trace, shortages, cutoff/settings, frozen probabilities and prematch_sp with input hashes, and role scores. SP quotes must be strictly before cutoff; absent historical SP is explicitly empty, never backfilled. Validate supplied score/rank/mask values against probabilities. Empty/insufficient pools return explicit shortages and typed empty tables. Internal role planning remains unexported.

No disk/network side effects or historical fitting.

Validate final selected portfolio constraints on every proposal; overlapping unselected candidates are allowed. ## Exploration-first research workflow (#542)

The original lab now constructs a named candidate pool for each structure before evaluating any configuration. `parlay_explore_portfolios(portfolios, scenarios, objective="probability")` evaluates explicit finite CNY caps, total budgets and reuse/overlap limits with improvement disabled, returning an input-aligned summary and evaluations named by unique scenario_id. All pools must have validated frozen contexts at the same issue/time, including when scenarios are empty. Fixed amounts and coverage fractions are converted to legal whole-ticket caps in the QMD; equivalent configurations share one evaluation while source requests retain their mapping.

Charts use actual cost, not available budget; coordinate deduplication is local to a structure/constraint/depth curve. The cost/coverage frontier compares nonempty configurations only under the same reuse/overlap limits. Selection occurs after exploration and is explicitly overridable by scenario_id. The default is a labelled research candidate based on expected winning tickets under a user-editable budget and constraint profile; it does not claim profit optimality or place bets. A selected configuration may then be improved using its original total-budget cap, which cannot exceed the selection ceiling. Missing historical prematchSP remains explicit; union hit probability, break-even probability and historical settlement validation remain unimplemented rather than approximated by ticket expectation.
